How to use this file: go section by section. Each section says **where to click** on LinkedIn, then gives the **text to paste**. Steps are for LinkedIn on a computer (desktop browser); the phone app has the same sections under the pencil icons.

Before you start:
- Open your profile: click **Me** (your photo, top right) → **View Profile**.
- Turn off update notifications so your network isn't pinged for every edit: when an edit box shows **"Share profile updates"** (or "Notify network"), switch it **Off**. Turn it **On** only for the final job/headline change if you want people to see it.

Rules followed: no salary, no link to the company GitHub, no COVID or offsite mentions.
